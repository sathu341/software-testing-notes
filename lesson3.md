# Quality Definition  
## Five Perspectives of Quality  
## Quality Assurance (QA) & Quality Control (QC)

---

## 1. What is Quality?

### Definition of Quality
**Quality** is the degree to which a product or service **meets customer requirements, expectations, and standards**.

> **Simple Definition:**  
Quality means **doing the right thing, in the right way, at the right time**.

---

### Quality in Software
In software, quality means:
- Correct functionality
- Reliability
- Performance
- Security
- Usability
- Maintainability

---

### Example
A **banking application** is considered high quality if:
- Transactions are accurate
- App is secure
- Works fast
- Easy to use
- No crashes

---

## 2. Five Perspectives of Quality

Quality can be viewed from **five different perspectives**.

---

### 1. Transcendent View of Quality
Quality is **excellence** that is recognized but difficult to define.

> "I know it when I see it"

#### Example
- Apple products
- High-end luxury cars

📌 Focus: **User perception**

---

### 2. Product-Based View of Quality
Quality is measured by **features and characteristics** of the product.

> More features = Higher quality

#### Example
- Mobile phone with:
  - High camera resolution
  - More RAM
  - Better battery

📌 Focus: **Measurable attributes**

---

### 3. User-Based View of Quality
Quality depends on **user satisfaction**.

> If the user is happy, the product is high quality.

#### Example
- Simple app preferred over complex app
- Elderly users prefer large buttons

📌 Focus: **User needs**

---

### 4. Manufacturing-Based View of Quality
Quality means **conformance to specifications**.

> Build the product exactly as designed.

#### Example
- Software meets all documented requirements
- No deviation from design

📌 Focus: **Process & standards**

---

### 5. Value-Based View of Quality
Quality is judged by **value for money**.

> Best quality at reasonable cost.

#### Example
- Budget smartphone with good features
- Free software with useful features

📌 Focus: **Cost vs benefit**

---

## 3. Quality Assurance (QA)

### What is Quality Assurance?
**Quality Assurance** is a **process-oriented approach** that focuses on **preventing defects** in software development.

> **QA answers:**  
"Are we following the correct process?"

---

### Characteristics of QA
- Preventive in nature
- Process focused
- Done throughout SDLC
- Improves development process

---

### QA Activities
- Defining standards
- Process documentation
- Training developers
- Audits
- Reviews
- Process improvement

---

### Example of QA
- Creating coding standards
- Defining testing strategy
- Conducting process audits

---

## 4. Quality Control (QC)

### What is Quality Control?
**Quality Control** is a **product-oriented approach** that focuses on **identifying defects** in the final product.

> **QC answers:**  
"Is the product working correctly?"

---

### Characteristics of QC
- Detective in nature
- Product focused
- Performed after development
- Involves testing activities

---

### QC Activities
- Manual testing
- Automation testing
- Defect tracking
- Regression testing

---

### Example of QC
- Testing login functionality
- Finding and reporting bugs

---

## 5. Quality Assurance vs Quality Control

| Feature | Quality Assurance (QA) | Quality Control (QC) |
|------|----------------------|--------------------|
| Focus | Process | Product |
| Nature | Preventive | Detective |
| Goal | Prevent defects | Find defects |
| Performed during | Entire SDLC | After development |
| Responsibility | Entire team | Testing team |
| Example | Process audits | Software testing |

---

## 6. Real-Time Example

### Online Food Delivery Application

#### Quality Assurance
- Define coding and testing standards
- Review requirement documents
- Conduct developer training

#### Quality Control
- Test order placement
- Validate payment process
- Check delivery tracking

---

## 7. Exam-Oriented Key Points
- Quality means meeting user requirements
- Five perspectives explain different views of quality
- QA prevents defects
- QC detects defects
- QA focuses on process, QC on product

---

## Summary
- Quality is customer satisfaction
- Different perspectives explain quality differently
- QA ensures correct processes
- QC ensures correct product
- Both are essential for software success
